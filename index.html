05

Klipper & OrcaSlicer Setup

01. Home All Axes

Click Home All (G28) in Fluidd first so Klipper establishes X0 Y0 Z0 before centering at X135 Y135 Z15.

02. Test in Fluidd Console

Select Fluidd Console, click Copy G-Code, paste into the Fluidd Console input box, and press Enter.

03. Save to printer.cfg

Switch to printer.cfg, paste [gcode_macro START_CHIME] at the bottom of your config, and hit Save & Restart.

04. Trigger in OrcaSlicer

Add START_CHIME inside Machine Start G-code right after G28 to play on every print start.
